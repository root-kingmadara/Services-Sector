Water Supply

Why It Matters

A Water Supply System Performs Only as Well as its Weakest Component, Whether that is the Source, Treatment, Storage, Pumping or the Pipe Network. Sound Design Begins with Realistic Peak and Average Demand, Provides for Fire Flow and Future Growth, and Balances Resilient Looped Networks Against Lower-Cost Branched Layouts. Pipe Material, Pressure Zoning and Storage Placement then Determine How Reliably Water Reaches every Consumer.

How We Work

We Survey the Source and Route, then Design the Intake, Treatment, Storage and Pipeline Network as one Integrated System. Bids, Estimates and DPRs are Supported at every Step.

Selected Experience

  • Drinking Water Supply Schemes for Begamganj & Gairatganj (M.P.) and Pondikhurd (C.G.): Survey, Design and Engineering for Intake, WTP, OHT and Pipeline Network
  • Water Supply and Sanitation DPRs for the Bhurkalkhapa (Seoni) and Bagaspur (Narsinghpur) Industrial Areas
  • Business Development Support for Water Supply Projects in Cambodia, Ghana and Laos

Water Treatment

Why It Matters

Conventional Treatment Moves Water through Screening, Coagulation, Flocculation, Sedimentation, Filtration and Disinfection, with each Stage Preparing the Water for the Next. The Design must Respond to the Quality of the Source: Groundwater Typically Calls for Iron and Manganese Removal, while Surface Water Calls for Turbidity and Algae Control. Flow Rates, Detention Times and Surface Loading Rates Decide Whether a Plant Performs as Intended.

How We Work

Process, Hydraulic, Structural and E&M Design, with Drawings and Layouts Ready for Construction. We also Design Renovations and Packaged Plants, Including RO and UF Systems.

Selected Experience

  • 20+ WTPs Designed, from 1 MLD to 20 MLD
  • Renovation of a 5.5 MLD WTP at Baherabandh, Anuppur (Mining Water)
  • Design and Drawings for a 1.5 MLD WTP at Rewa and a 4 MLD WTP at Maneri, Jabalpur

Sewage Treatment

Why It Matters

Selecting the Right Process is the Most Important Design Decision. SBR Carries Out every Stage in a Single Tank in Sequence. MBBR Uses Floating Media that Carry Active Bacteria, and Offers a Compact Footprint with Low Operating Cost. MBR Combines Biological Treatment with Membrane Filtration, Producing the Highest-Quality Effluent for Reuse but Requiring Regular Membrane Maintenance. The Choice Depends on Available Space, Discharge Standards and Reuse Needs.

How We Work

We Select the Process to Suit the Site and the Client’s Requirements, Prepare Hydraulic and Structural Designs, and Design the Sewer Pipelines that Serve each Plant.

Selected Experience

  • MBBR-Based STPs of 0.70, 0.60 and 0.35 MLD at Bijuri, Anuppur
  • 10 STPs Designed, and about 1,000 km of Sewer Pipeline Hydraulic Design
  • Sewage Pipeline Design for the Bagaspur Industrial Area, Narsinghpur

Irrigation

Why It Matters

Only 23% of the World’s Cropland is Irrigated, Yet that Land Produces 48% of Global Crop Value, with Yields about 76% Higher than Rainfed Land. More than 60% of Irrigated Land Already Faces High or Very High Water Stress, and Climate Change could Raise Agricultural Water Demand by 20–30% by 2050. In this Setting, Careful Canal and Distribution Design is Essential, so that every Cubic Metre of Water Delivered is Used Well.

How We Work

Pre- and Post-Bid Engineering, Topographical and Contour Survey, Detailed Design of Canals and Distribution Networks, and Tendering and Cost Estimates.

Selected Experience

  • ONG Irrigation Expansion Project, Subarnapur, Odisha: 10,252 ha Command Area
  • Distribution System for 1,500 ha of the Naren Distributary, Indira Irrigation Project (UGPL), Balangir, Odisha
  • Namxang Dam & Irrigation Project (1,040 ha), Laos

Dams & Barrages

Why It Matters

A 2025 Study of 33,886 Large Dams in the ICOLD World Register Found that 53% are more than 50 Years Old and 7% are more than a Century Old. Age Alone has not Yet Raised Failure Rates, but Flood-Driven Failures have Doubled or Tripled in Recent Decades, and about 4.4% of Large Dams Exceed the Standard Safety Threshold of a 1 in 10,000 Annual Failure Probability. Flood Studies, Stability Analysis and Dam Break Analysis are Therefore Central to Dam Engineering, in New Structures and Existing Ones Alike.

How We Work

Hydraulic Studies, Structural Design and Stability Analysis, Dam Break Analysis and Flood Management Planning, Plus Support for Bids and Project Reports.

Selected Experience

  • Namxang Dam, Laos: 30 m High and 220 m Long. Survey, Planning, Contract Documentation, Estimates and Detailed Design
  • Brahmani Barrage, Rajasthan: Pre-Bid Engineering, Tender Estimates and Land Acquisition Planning
  • Yamuna Nagar Weir for Groundwater Recharge, a Stop Dam at Shahdol, and the Bagh River Diversion Project

Road & Bridge

Why It Matters

A Bridge is the Result of a Sequence of Decisions: Feasibility and Planning, Detailed Design, Permits, Site Preparation, Foundations and Substructure, Superstructure, Finishing, Load Testing and, Once Open, Continuous Maintenance. Foundation Choices Follow Site Geology, and Inspection and Testing Confirm Structural Integrity before the Structure is Opened to Traffic. Roads and Bridges Perform Best where Alignment, Drainage and Structures are Designed Together.

How We Work

Alignment Survey, Road Network Design, and Detailed Engineering of Bridges, Culverts and Minor Bridges.

Selected Experience

  • Road, Bridge, Culvert and Minor Bridge Works at WRD Banda, Sagar (M.P.)
  • A 350 m Approach Bridge and a 400 m Bituminous Road for the Bhurkalkhapa Water Supply Scheme
  • 100 km of Road Design to Date

Railway

Why It Matters

A Railway is Built in Layers: A Prepared Formation, Subgrade, Sub-Ballast and Ballast, then Sleepers, Fastenings and Rails. Subgrade Stiffness, Drainage (Ditches, Culverts and Outlets) and Slope Stability Keep the Track Aligned and Safe. Depots and Workshops Need Layouts that Minimise Movements, Separate Tracks for Light and Heavy Maintenance, Dedicated Areas for Electrical Work, and Room for Staged Expansion.

How We Work

Structural Design and Detailed Drawings for Railway Buildings and Depots.

Selected Experience

  • Railway Shed, OHE Depot, PSI Depot and Tower Wagon Shed at Pendra, Bilaspur (C.G.), Currently Ongoing
  • Business Development Support for Projects with Railway Clients in India
